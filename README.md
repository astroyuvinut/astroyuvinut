## About

- I build retrieval systems, decision engines, and orbital-mechanics tooling. Mostly Python and TypeScript, mostly shipped end to end rather than left in a notebook.
- Founder of **Akasha**, early-stage research on post-quantum authentication for bandwidth-limited satellite links.
- AI/ML engineering intern at **Sprintm** (2024 to 2025).
- Third year B.Tech CSE (AI) at KIET Kakinada, JNTUK, graduating May 2028. Competitive programmer, freelance on Fiverr.
- Currently exploring: vector retrieval at scale, post-quantum cryptography, and real-time server-authoritative architectures.

## Featured Projects

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **[WarehouseOS](https://github.com/astroyuvinut/warehouseos)** | Operations decision engine that scores orders, allocates scarce inventory without breaking picked work, and optimizes pick routes. Cuts walking distance 42% against a FIFO baseline on the seeded dataset, 62% at 200 orders. Every decision is audited and explained. | Next.js, TypeScript, SQLite, SSE |
| **[ReelRAG](https://github.com/astroyuvinut/ReelRAG)** | Side-by-side RAG chatbot for comparing two videos. Local BGE-small embeddings and ChromaDB, so retrieval costs nothing. Streams cited answers down to the source chunk. | FastAPI, React, LangChain, Gemini, ChromaDB |
| **[Satellite Pass Predictor](https://github.com/astroyuvinut/astro--the-space-expo)** | Computes when a given satellite will be overhead and visible from your coordinates, using live TLE data from Celestrak. | Python, Skyfield, NumPy, Streamlit |
| **[BLEACH](https://github.com/astroyuvinut/BLEACH)** | ChatGPT-style chat app with automatic Gemini to Groq failover. Falls back on timeouts, rate limits and 5xx, not on malformed requests. | React, Node, Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL |
| **[Gauss Elimination Simulator](https://github.com/astroyuvinut/gauss-elimination-simulator)** | Watch Gauss-Jordan row operations play out on three planes in 3D. | Python, Manim, Gradio |
